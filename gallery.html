There is a life here beyond the attendance register.

We ask a great deal of our students — ninety per cent attendance, uniform on duty, night shifts. We also make room for Ganeshotsav, sports day, cooking competitions and a proper convocation. Both are true, and both belong on this page.

Training

Classroom and practical work

The first four months at the Dadar premises: lectures, practical laboratory sessions and the chapter-end tests that count towards internal assessment.

Hospital postings

On duty across Mumbai

From the fifth month, students work full shifts in nursing homes, diagnostic laboratories, operation theatres and opticians' practices under a practising doctor.

More about postings and placement
Campus life

Festivals, competitions and celebration

Funded entirely from fine collections — not one rupee of it is used by the institute for itself. Ganeshotsav, Dassehra, Diwali, Eid, Christmas, New Year, Republic Day and Gurupurnima are all marked here.

Competitions & sports

Beyond the syllabus

Activities run alongside the course and included in the fee — no separate charge.

Convocation

The day the diploma is awarded

Held in August or September each year, subject to results and hall availability. Certificates are awarded under Bharat Sevak Samaj certification, and families are welcome.

A note on these photographs

The practical and posting photographs on this page are from the institute's own archive, taken at the practices where our students train. Important disclosures. Placement assistance offered by the institute covers private hospitals, clinics and diagnostic laboratories only, and does not extend to appointments in Government hospitals. The Diploma in Patient Care (DPC) and Diploma in Patient Care Assistant (DPCA) have no affiliation with the Indian Nursing Council. Certificate courses for working technicians are non-stipendiary and are not covered by placement assistance. Stipend amounts are paid by the host nursing home, laboratory or clinic and may vary according to the student's work. Fees, instalment dates and refund terms are as stated in the printed prospectus, which forms the binding record.
