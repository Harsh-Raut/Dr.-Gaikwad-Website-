01

Executive summary

The institute has a genuinely strong story. Almost none of it is on the website.

Strip away the marketing language and the prospectus describes something uncommon: a government-linked (BSS) vocational institute where fees are structured to be recovered through a rising monthly stipend, with a scoped-but-real 100% job guarantee in the private sector and a claimed 10,000-placement, thirty-year track record. That is a strong, specific pitch to a 10th/12th-pass family weighing career options in Mumbai.

The live site at drgaikwadinstitute.com does not tell this story — it tells a different one entirely. The About page describes an unrelated wellness-coaching business with testimonials from hotel executives; the services menu lists Fire & Safety training and cosmetology instead of the four real diplomas; there is no fee information, no admission form that works, and no BSS credibility markers anywhere. Full findings in Section 02.

The recommendation is not a redesign in the cosmetic sense. It's a rebuild of the site around the facts already sitting in the printed prospectus — fees, stipend schedule, eligibility, refund rules, accreditation — served through the same technical approach used on prior builds (custom-coded, Netlify-hosted), paired with a marketing plan built around the channel that actually closes admissions in this market: WhatsApp, not a contact form.

03

Brand & positioning

Lead with the outcome, not the curriculum. This is a job-placement pipeline that happens to teach.

The prospectus itself is built this way: fees net out close to zero once the stipend is counted, the headline claim is "100% job guarantee," and the institute tracks placements, not just enrolments. The website should mirror that priority order — job first, course second, curriculum detail third.

Four messaging pillars, each anchored to a fact already in the prospectus

Earn while you learn

Stipend rises ₹2,000 → ₹3,000 → ₹4,000 → ₹5,000 across the four-month blocks of a twenty-month diploma, offsetting most or all of the fee.

A track record you can check

30 years, 10,000+ placements claimed, zero job-less graduating batches per the institute's own account — worth verifying and then stating plainly, with numbers, not adjectives.

Government-linked, not a garage operation

BSS was established under the Planning Commission in 1952 with a constitution unanimously approved by Parliament. Genuinely rare for a private diploma institute — currently the most under-used asset on the site.

Discipline as a selling point

The strict attendance, dress-code and conduct rules read harsh as fine print. Reframed as "how we run a professional training floor," they become a credibility signal for parents, not a warning label.

Conversion funnel

The site's job is not to explain everything — it's to remove the two objections ("is the fee real money?" and "can I trust this place?") fast enough that the visitor picks up the phone or opens WhatsApp.

08

Technical specification

Stack decision

Keep the known-good approach: a statically generated site (Next.js or Astro) deployed on Netlify, matching prior builds so deploys, previews and rollbacks need no new operational learning. The change is in the content layer, not the hosting model.

Why fees and dates cannot be hard-coded

The prospectus itself proves this point — it bakes in a payment schedule running to specific dates through April 2028. Those numbers change every admission cycle. Course, fee, and date data need to live in a lightweight content layer (a git-based CMS such as Decap CMS, editable by non-developers) rather than inside page components, or every fee revision becomes a developer ticket.

SEO strategy

Local search first

Claim and fully build out the Google Business Profile — categories, service area (Dadar, and the posting catchment up to Virar/Kalyan/Chembur that the institute already commits to), photos, and an ongoing review request sent to every newly placed student. For a single-location institute, this listing will outperform the website itself for near-term "course near me" searches.

On-page & technical checklist

  • Unique, keyword-specific title and meta description per page — replacing the current generic, non-education title tag
  • Descriptive alt text on every posting/facility photo
  • Internal linking: Home → each course page → Fees, so authority flows to the pages that convert
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt, plus fixing the current 404 on the contact path

Content pillars for an ongoing blog/FAQ

  • "DMLT vs. DPC: which diploma after 10th" — comparison content captures undecided-searcher traffic
  • "How the stipend actually works" — walks through the real ₹2,000→₹5,000 ladder, addresses skepticism directly
  • "What BSS is, and why it matters for this diploma" — turns the accreditation into searchable trust content
  • Timed post each May/June: "Results are out — how to choose a paramedical course," published to catch the board-result search spike (see Section 10 calendar)

Off-page

Listings on high-relevance Indian education directories (Shiksha, Sulekha, JustDial), a BSS-affiliate directory listing if one exists, and backlinks from any nameable partner hospitals/labs. Alumni are a real, untapped source of both backlinks and reviews at 10,000+ claimed placements.

Admission-cycle calendar

The prospectus's own installment and refund rules imply a roughly June intake with a 25 September late-admission/refund cutoff. Marketing intensity should track that shape, not run flat all year — confirm the exact recurring dates with the institute before locking media spend.

11

Content & compliance requirements

Items only the institute can provide, needed before build starts on the pages that depend on them.

  • One verified founding year. The certificate badge implies roughly three decades of operation; the current site says 2013. These need to reconcile to a single true number before it appears anywhere.
  • Placement statistics. Year-by-year if trackable, or the general "10,000+ over 30 years" figure kept consistent everywhere it's used.
  • Consent to publish student photos, names, and testimonials — including the posting photos already shown in the printed prospectus.
  • BSS registration/certificate documents for on-page display and schema markup.
  • Sign-off on scope disclosures — DPC/DPCA has no Nursing Council affiliation, and the job guarantee excludes government hospitals. These must appear on-site as clearly as they already appear in the printed prospectus, both for honesty and because Google/Meta can suspend education advertisers over unverifiable job-guarantee claims.
  • A ruling on the old site's "services." Confirm whether Fire & Safety, Competitive Exam coaching, Cosmetology, and foreign languages are real current offerings to carry forward, or template leftovers to drop.
