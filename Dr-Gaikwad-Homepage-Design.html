Admissions

How to join

Four steps from first enquiry to your first day of classroom training. Our counsellors will confirm your eligibility before you pay anything.

STEP 01

Enquire

Call, or message us on WhatsApp with your name, the course you are considering, and your last examination result. We reply the same working day.

STEP 02

Counselling

Meet us at Dadar with a parent or guardian. We explain the course, the posting system, the full fee and stipend schedule, and answer every question before you commit.

STEP 03

Submit documents

Mark sheets, Aadhaar, leaving certificate, domicile, medical fitness certificate and photographs in white uniform against a white background.

STEP 04

Confirm admission

Pay the first instalment to reserve your seat. Classroom training begins with your batch; hospital posting follows in the fifth month.

Documents

What to bring

10th / 12th mark sheet (3 copies) · Aadhaar card (1) · School or college leaving certificate (2) · Caste certificate (2, if applicable) · Domicile certificate (1) · Medical fitness certificate (1) · 10 colour photographs.

Postings

Where you will train

Nursing homes, diagnostic laboratories, clinics and opticians across Mumbai — as close to your residence as we can arrange, up to Virar, Kalyan and Chembur. Parents are welcome to visit the posting with the student.

Refund policy

Stated in writing

5% deduction if you withdraw before the course commences; 10% within seven days; 25% within one month. No refund after one month from commencement, or for admissions taken after 25 September. Refunds are issued by cheque.

The certification behind your diploma

A credential with a constitutional record.

Bharat Sevak Samaj is a national development agency promoted by the Government of India in 1952 to secure public co-operation in implementing government plans. Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru was its founder President, and its constitution and functioning were approved unanimously by the Indian Parliament — recorded in the First Five Year Plan, in the chapter on public co-operation in national development.

Every diploma awarded at this institute is issued under that certification. For three decades we have trained students in Dadar under it, and placed more than ten thousand of them in hospitals, clinics and laboratories across Mumbai.
